Map building · Pet Simulator style

Crystal Meadow zone

A sample simulator zone I built as a layout reference: a closed meadow ringed by two-tier cliffs, a curved path from the entrance to a locked Zone 2 gate, open fields for breakables and an egg station you can see from the entrance.

  • 170 × 230 stud playable area, entrance courtyard and exit corridor
  • 26 breakables in 6 fields, kept clear of the path and the water
  • Egg station on a raised platform with stairs, lanterns and price label
  • Locked Zone 2 gate with barrier and unlock price, ready for a purchase script
  • VFX: layered waterfall with mist and rainbow, pollen, stream glints, coin sparkles, snow drifting from the Zone 2 gate
  • 70 reused meshes, no unions, free assets scanned for backdoors
  • Scripted placement check: nothing floating, buried, overlapping or on the path
